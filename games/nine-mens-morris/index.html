Nine Men's Morris

Place stones, close three-in-a-row mills, then slide to trap the rival board.

Turn: Ivory Place: 9 - 9 Ivory places the first stone.
Ivory: 0 on board Green: 0 on board Tap a point to place or select. A mill lets you remove one rival stone.
